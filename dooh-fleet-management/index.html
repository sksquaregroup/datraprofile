FAQ

DOOH fleet management, answered.

What is DOOH fleet management software?
It gives operators one platform to monitor, control and maintain a large estate of digital out-of-home displays — uptime monitoring, remote access, diagnostics, alerting and proof of play across every location.
How does Datra manage a large display estate?
Your whole estate appears on a live, colour-coded map. Drill from estate level to any unit, and use remote terminal, diagnostics and alerting to resolve most issues from the office rather than a site visit.
Does Datra replace my content management system?
No. Datra is a management and monitoring layer over your existing CMS — Broadsign, Dise, Scala, NowSignage and others — so there's no rip-and-replace.
