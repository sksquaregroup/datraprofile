Platform Capabilities

Everything your operation
runs on, in one place.

A walk through the core of Unity — the same tools your team uses every day to keep an estate up, verified and under control.

Estate Map

Your whole estate at a glance.

Every location on one interactive map with colour-coded pins — green online, red offline, grey dormant. Drill from estate level into any site or individual unit in seconds, with hotspot analysis surfacing the worst performers automatically.

app.unityplatform.com/dashboard
Visual Monitoring

Every display as a live tile.

A tile-based view of every screen, each showing its most recent screenshot with timestamp — and camera capture where fitted. Spot a black screen or the wrong content instantly, without opening a single unit.

app.unityplatform.com/units
Remote Control

Fix issues in minutes, not site visits.

Browser-based terminal, SSH, remote desktop and capture on any device — plus an 82-command RS232 & PC library for power, brightness, volume and input. No VPN, no callout, full audit trail of every session.

app.unityplatform.com/units · Commands
Analytics & Alerting

Trends, diagnostics and alerts.

Network-wide uptime, MTBF, MTTR and incidents trended over time, nine diagnostic modules per unit, and customised alerting that catches offline screens, stale content and power issues — on your thresholds, to your team.

app.unityplatform.com/analytics
