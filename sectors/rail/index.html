FAQ

Rail signage, answered.

How does Datra manage rail digital signage?
It monitors every concourse and platform display on a live estate map, with remote terminal access, diagnostics, alerting and proof of play, so failures are caught and fixed before passengers or advertisers are affected.
Can Datra help meet advertising SLAs on rail networks?
Yes. Proof of play and uptime trends confirm advertising ran as contracted, and exportable reports provide the evidence needed at SLA reviews.
Does Datra work across a large rail network?
Yes. The estate map shows every station and unit, and remote tools resolve most issues centrally — without dispatching staff to each station.
