FAQ

Airport signage, answered.

How does Unity manage airport digital signage?
It monitors every display across the airport — departure boards, wayfinding and concession media — on a live estate map, with remote terminal access, diagnostics, alerting and proof of play, so issues are caught and fixed before passengers notice.
Can Unity verify what is showing on airport screens?
Yes. Live screenshots and, where cameras are fitted, footage of the physical display confirm the right content is on screen — important for flight information accuracy and concession advertising.
Does Unity support inspection and compliance reporting?
Yes. Structured pre- and post-checks, photos and tasks build an evidence trail for planned maintenance and compliance across terminal display estates.
