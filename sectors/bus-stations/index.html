FAQ

Bus station signage, answered.

How does Datra manage bus station digital signage?
It brings every interchange and stop display into one platform — a live estate map, remote terminal, diagnostics and alerting — so passenger information stays live and most faults are fixed without a site visit.
Can Datra reduce engineer visits to bus stops?
Yes. Remote terminal, capture and the command library resolve most issues from the control room, which is especially valuable across dispersed bus estates.
Does Datra track advertising on interchange displays?
Yes. Proof of play confirms advertising ran across interchange screens, supporting media reporting and accountability.
