FAQ

Retail signage, answered.

How does Unity manage retail digital signage?
It keeps every store and centre screen on-message and online — visual monitoring, remote fixes, proof of play and reporting — so content stays consistent and verified across the retail estate.
Can Unity protect brand consistency across stores?
Yes. Live screenshots verify the right content is playing on every screen, and proof of play gives brand and franchise partners confidence their content is running as agreed.
Does Unity suit dispersed in-store networks?
Yes. The whole estate is managed from one platform, with remote fixes that avoid store-by-store visits across a multi-site retail network.
