FAQ

Roadside & OOH, answered.

How does Datra support proof of play for OOH billing?
Datra pairs your CMS content schedule with live screenshots and camera capture, producing timestamped evidence that the creative was genuinely on the physical screen — exactly what advertiser billing and disputes require.
How does Datra protect billable uptime on roadside screens?
Automatic alerting detects offline and stale screens quickly, so faults are fixed before billable hours are lost, and uptime trends evidence performance to advertisers.
Can Datra reduce callouts to roadside sites?
Yes. Remote terminal, capture and an 82-command library resolve most issues centrally, cutting the need to dispatch engineers to roadside locations.
