What Proof of Play Means

Dispatched isn't the same
as displayed.

A content management system can confirm a file was scheduled and sent. It can't confirm the screen wasn't black, frozen, showing the wrong creative, or physically obscured. True proof of play closes that gap — and protects the revenue that depends on it.

Verified on screen
On-demand and scheduled screenshots confirm the right creative was actually rendering on each display.
Camera-confirmed
Where IP cameras are fitted, stream the physical screen live to prove real-world conditions — not just what the player thinks it's doing.
Evidence for clients
Timestamped capture and schedule history give you exportable proof to back billing and resolve advertiser disputes.

FAQ

Proof of play, answered.

What is proof of play in digital signage?
Evidence that scheduled content actually played on a screen — verified by Unity with live screenshots and, where cameras are fitted, footage of the physical display. Not just a log that content was dispatched.
How does Unity prove content played on the physical screen?
It captures on-demand and scheduled screenshots of each display and pairs them with your CMS schedule. Where IP cameras are installed, it can stream the physical screen to confirm real-world conditions.
Does proof of play work with my existing CMS?
Yes. Unity sits as a monitoring layer over Broadsign, Dise, Scala, NowSignage and others, reading the schedule and verifying playback without replacing your CMS.
