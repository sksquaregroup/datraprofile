Interactive Estate Overview

Understand your estate
health at a glance.

The Datra dashboard puts your whole network on one interactive map. Location groups appear as colour-coded pins, so you can read the health of hundreds of sites instantly — then zoom straight into the ones that need attention.

Colour-coded location pins
Green for all units online, red for one or more offline, grey for dormant groups. Estate health in a single glance.

Getting Started

Live on your network
in four steps.

Datra deploys as a monitoring and management layer over your existing CMS — Broadsign, Dise, Scala, or anything else. No rip-and-replace, no downtime.

1
Connect Your Displays
Install the lightweight Datra agent on media players — or, for Samsung & LG smart signage, simply point the built-in display at the platform. No external PC, no agent.
2
Connect to Cloud
Units auto-register under your Organisation ID and begin reporting. Outbound connection only — no inbound firewall changes.
3
Map Your Estate
Group units by location, assign coordinates, and configure alerts. Your live map and Classic dashboard populate instantly.
4
Take Control
Remote terminal, diagnostics, screenshots, and command library are active from day one — across your whole network.
