How Datra Works

One platform. One agent.
Complete control.

Datra connects your signage devices to a central web platform through the lightweight Datra Agent, installed on each player.

Install the Agent
A lightweight install on your signage PC or media player. Runs quietly in the background, securely connecting the device to Datra. (Windows • Linux • other supported environments)
Connect Your Devices
Each device becomes a managed unit, reporting system info, resource usage, network status, temperature, service health, and diagnostics back to the platform.
Monitor From Web
See your whole network organized by Locations → Groups → Devices → Individual Player, with clear Online and Offline status at every level.
Detect Issues Early
Continuous monitoring flags problems as they emerge — offline players, stalled services, high temperatures, network drops, hardware faults, content issues — before they become complaints.
Troubleshoot Remotely
Investigate directly from the platform: screenshots, camera verification, logs, remote terminal, commands and jobs, service actions, remote control. No switching tools mid-incident.
Resolve & Verify
Take the action, confirm the fix, and let the Agent report the updated status back — closing the loop without a site visit.
From device to dashboard — Datra keeps your signage operation connected, visible, and under control.
