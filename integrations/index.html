FAQ

Integrations, answered.

Does Unity Platform have an API?
Yes — a REST API with scoped API keys lets you read units, locations, jobs and reports and integrate estate data with your own dashboards, BI and internal systems.
Can Unity send events to Slack, Teams or our ticketing system?
Yes. Outbound webhooks push events such as offline units, stale screens and faults to external systems in real time, fitting Unity into your existing workflow.
Does Unity support single sign-on (SSO)?
Yes — enterprise SSO alongside role-based access and per-tenant isolation keeps access centrally controlled and auditable.
Which CMS and hardware does Unity integrate with?
Unity sits over Broadsign, Dise, Scala and NowSignage, and manages displays from Samsung, LG, Philips, Daktronics, Absen, NovaStar and more — across Windows, Linux, macOS, BrightSign and Android.
